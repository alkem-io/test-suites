# Role sets (membership, applications, invitations, associates): behaviour rules

_What the platform does today for the role sets of spaces, organisations and the platform: the roles and their limits, the entry role and the rule that a subspace member is a member of the parent, adding and removing people directly, applications, invitations to existing users, organisations and virtual contributors, email invitations, organisation associates, joining through a matching email domain, the combined application or join for a subspace, Platform Support's audited work on organisations, and the lists of pending requests. The file follows the three-section layout accepted in the product review of `communications.md` (alkem-io/test-suites#717): **1. Rules**, **2. Rights**, **3. Open questions**, with the evidence in an **Appendix**._

_Open questions per owner: Product Lead 3 · Technical Lead 4 · Quality Lead 8 · Security Lead 1 · Incident Lead 2. By state: 12 open · 6 with a suggested answer · 11 decided. Proof of the 79 rules: 60 proven by a test · 19 untested (Appendix A)._

_Whys of the 79 rules: 55 rest on a source · 17 carry a draft for product to decide · 7 have none, and each already carries an open question or a defect (RS-04: F1; RS-06: F2; RS-27: Q-RS-02; RS-36: F4; RS-54: F11; RS-77: F7; RS-79: F13)._

**How to read it**

- **Rule** is one or two plain sentences. The exact behaviour, with error messages and limits, is in Appendix A under the same id. The new core is checked against the exact text.
- **Why: business** is what Alkemio needs. **Why: product** is the user story. **Why: technical** is the engineering reason. A rule needs at least one why, and gets a second or third only where it adds something for the decision or the code. "—" means none is given.
- A why that names a spec, issue, pull request, code comment or code line rests on that source. A why, or the part of a why, ending in _(draft)_ was written by QA and no source states it: product keeps, rewrites or deletes it, and sets **Decided by**. A user story always ends in its "so that"; where no source gives one, QA wrote it. A rule with no why at all carries an open question in section 3.
- **Decided by** names the source of the decision: a spec with its requirement, a pull request or commit, an issue or a document. "Not decided: exists in the code" means the behaviour exists in the code and nobody chose it. "Not decided: a defect" means today's behaviour is a fault.
- A marker in bold at the end of a rule points at section 3: **(open: F8)** means a question on this rule is still open, **(defect: F2)** means today's behaviour is a fault and must not be copied as intended, **(defect in part: F5)** means part of it is. A rule with no marker stands as written. Every rule is logged as the server behaves today, also when that is not the intended end state. Whether a test proves the rule sits in Appendix A as **proven** or **untested**.
- Hard rules are not marked yet. Some rules are choices that a new feature may challenge, and some can never change; a later version will mark the second kind with "(hard rule)" after the why that makes it so. Until then, no marker does not mean a rule is open to change.
- A decided question leaves section 3 and moves onto its rule; "was F4" in **Decided by** keeps the trail. Nothing is decided in this file yet.
- Words used here: a "top-level space" is what the appendix calls L0, a subspace L1 and a sub-subspace L2; "the right to grant roles" is GRANT; an "actor" is a user, an organisation or a virtual contributor; "acceptance" is the ACC environment.

### C. Findings and questions in full

_The original text of every finding (F) and open question (OQ), with its evidence and the ruling it asks for. The text writes "F-1" or "finding 1" for F1 and "open question 2" for OQ2. The rule-level questions (Q-…) were added in the conversion and have no longer text._

#### Findings

- **F1 — "Requires the entry role" is declared but never enforced (RS-04).** Code: no check on the assign path; the flag only filters the elevated-role candidate list. Test: suites routinely make personas admin-not-associate / owner-not-admin and nothing refuses it. Spec 062: "each requiring the entry role by declaration though not enforced at assignment". Register: A5 (67 space lead/admin without member, 45 of them organisations), A6 (757 owner/admin without associate). Ruling: enforce (and repair), or drop the flag and document "admin need not be a member".
- **F2 — Removing a role that is not held is refused at the minimum, silently accepted otherwise (RS-06).** Code: the minimum is compared with the current holder count, not with whether the actor holds the role. Test: only a teardown comment. Spec: none. Ruling: removing an unheld role should be a no-op (idempotent like assign) — rebaseline the error.
- **F3 — Organisation invited to L1 ends up in L2; removal from L2 strips L1 (RS-18, RS-52; server#6710, open).** Code: the ancestor walk grants upward only and the cascade revokes downward only, so neither explains it; the issue's own pointers suspect a read-side artefact. Test: `invitation-organization-subspace` proves the L2 → [L0, L1, L2] case; no case pins L1 → not-L2 or L2-removal-keeps-L1. Ruling: reproduce (dev note: not reproduced locally 2026-10-23, real defect there was a stale membership cache after an incomplete removal) and pin.
- **F4 — Invitee standing is never withdrawn on decline or revoke (RS-36; server#6731, register A9).** Code: only the accept path removes `space-invitee`. Test: none reads the credential. Spec 061/081: silent. Ruling: revoke on delete/reject (and on account deletion), then purge the 56 stale rows.
- **F5 — Parent credentials point at the old L0 after a move (RS-74; server#6592, register A3).** Code: the move re-parents without the roles loaded, so `setParentRoleSetAndCredentials` writes nothing. Test: `journey/conversion/move-*` do not read `parentCredentials`. Spec 030 FR-005 says they must be consistent. Ruling: fix; and the ETL must not translate stored `parentCredentials` into grants.
- **F6 — 147 subspace admin roles carry an ancestor's member credential as parent (RS-76; register A1, crosscheck F7).** Code writes the same-named parent credential (admin → admin); the rows are legacy data. Ruling: data repair on ACC and PROD before cutover; the matrix cells for `space-member@L0` on L1/L2 resources are the target.
- **F7 — Stored role definitions drift from the code (RS-02, RS-77; crosscheck F4).** Code: caps in the definitions files. Data: VC caps 9/0, L0 org-lead 9, member same-role-in-parent `true` on 11 L0 rows. Test: `organization-edge` › `Error is thrown for Space` is skipped and expects an org-lead cap of **1** (code: 2, some stored rows: 9). Ruling: one cap per (role, actor type, level) for the new core; decide the VC lead/member caps and whether organisations may lead an L0 at all.
- **F8 — A rejected request stays "open" on a space but not on an organisation (RS-26, RS-37).** Code: deliberate, scoped to organisations with a comment; space keeps archive-then-re-invite. Test: both halves proven on their own side; the space "rejected still blocks" half is not pinned. Spec 061 R4 documented the space route as archive-first. Ruling: with one `membership_request` the new core needs one answer — does a decline/rejection end the request?
- **F9 — An approved application produces the generic "new member joined" but no application-approved event (RS-31; server#6476, open).** Code: `CommunityMembershipOrigin` has no `APPLICATION` member; 061 R40 restored R31. 062 FR-010 wants "someone joined" on approval for organisations (consistent) and R48 leaves it an open product question. Ruling: add the approved event (then suppress) or keep.
- **F10 — Lead downgrade is silent on spaces, typed on organisations (RS-39, RS-50 vs RS-56).** Code: the space path catches and logs; the organisation path reports `extraRolesWithheld`. Spec 061 R5 accepted the silent downgrade; 062 SC-001 forbids silent downgrades on organisations. Ruling: one behaviour for the new core (typed on both).
- **F11 — Owner minimum of 1 is only enforced on removal (RS-54; register E2).** Code: creation grants associate + admin, never owner; 126 ACC organisations have no owner, 40 neither owner nor admin, 11 of 23 2026 organisations no roles at all (created without an actor). Spec 062: "Owner min 1" authoritative. Ruling: creator = owner, or drop the minimum.
- **F12 — The subspace "allow Platform Support as admin" switch is inert (RS-78; crosscheck F8).** Code: subspace Support rights follow the parent. UI exposes the switch per space. Ruling: hide it below L0 or honour it.
- **F13 — Direct assignment of organisation associates through the API (RS-79; server#6608, open).** Code: organisation admins/owners hold entry-assign; the interface removed "add associate" (062 R47 "confirm with operator"); 090 closed the path for Support only. Ruling: organisation self-management, or invitation-only like spaces.
- **F14 — Owners who are not admins may accept on the organisation's behalf but are never notified; the zero-admin notice ignores owners (RS-46, RS-51).** Code: `ORGANIZATION_NOTIFICATION_CREDENTIAL_TYPES = [organization-admin]` by product email (061 R17b); the spec lists it as an open product question. Ruling: notify owners too, or document.
- **F15 — The harness admin's "join fallback" hides the L0 direct-add refusal.** Tests: 18 `assign user as member` cases in `user*.it-spec.ts` and the organisation assign cases run as the harness admin, whose helper falls back to join/invite when entry-assign is refused (`user.authorization.it-spec.ts:1781-1785`, `organization.it-spec.ts:358-364`), so those cases prove the holder lists, not the gate. ### F. Where this lands in the new core

The briefing folds the community and role set into **credentials on the space** and the organisation (one `credential` row per actor × resource × role, no derived `space-subspace-admin` / `account-admin` rows and no stored `parentCredentials` — the hierarchy is walked by the SQL verdict), applications and invitations of every kind into **one `membership_request`** with a direction (in = application, out = invitation) and a plain state, the membership policy and the "allow subspace admins to invite" switch into `credential_allowed_role` rows, and the stored authorization policies and every reset mutation into a single SQL verdict over the rule tables (catalogue MB-1…MB-9, slices 6–7). Every rule above stays valid as a behaviour; what changes is that RS-07/RS-76 stop being data (A1/A3/A7 vanish), the lifecycle states (RS-23/RS-37) collapse to the request's state, the rejected-but-open asymmetry (F-8) must be decided rather than inherited, the email (platform) invitation tokens are a continuity item at cutover, the Support audit rows (RS-60) must be re-homed in the ops namespace, and the six platform assignment rules (RS-71) become constraints on credential writes. The `_inventory` cases above are the acceptance backlog for slices 6–7; the four open defects (F-3, F-4, F-5, F-13) must be settled before the ETL copies the credentials they shaped.
