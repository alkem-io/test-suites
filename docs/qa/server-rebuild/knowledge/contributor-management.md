# Contributor management — behaviour knowledge

_Extracted 2026-11-06 from three sources: server develop @ `eeecf9ddb` (Release/79) code + `schema.graphql`; the legacy server-api suite (`server-api/src/functional-api/contributor-management/`, 16 files, 290 cases per `_inventory.md` (292 test blocks by body count), 1 skipped baseline gate; 200 of them are the two `test.each` privilege tables of the virtual-contributor access files); the specs, issues and docs listed in § Sources. Scope: users, organisations and virtual contributors as contributors — how they come into being, their profiles, settings, verification, model cards, deletion, lookup and who may see their personal data. Space/organisation membership, invitations, licensing (entitlements), notifications and the `account-deletion` client-web walks are their own areas and are only touched where a contributor action changes them. WHAT and WHY only — no mutations, fixtures or request params. Status legend: **proven** = a green legacy case asserts it; **untested** = code and/or spec only, no case; **disputed** = sources disagree, see § Findings; **unknown-why** = the rule holds but no source states the reason — the reviewer fills the why or writes "unknown, ask product". Evidence paths are relative to the server repo's `src/` unless prefixed `test ›` or `spec ›`._

_Review load: 14 findings · 7 unknown-why rows · 16 untested rows (gaps) · 43 constants to tick · 5 open questions. Manual points sit in five places: the Findings, the `unknown-why` rows, the `untested` rows (repeated under Gaps), the "product must rule?" column of the constants, and the Open questions._

## Where this lands in the new core

Users, organisations and virtual contributors all become `actor` rows (kind user / organisation / vc, with the assistant as a fourth kind) — the catalogue's ID-3, MB-7 and AI-1 rows — read through `viewer` for the self case and `node(id)` for everyone else; profile fields, references and tagsets become vocabulary + label rows, preferences become `user_settings` attribute rows (CM-19..22 keep their semantics but the per-row merge and the `@AfterLoad` backstop become a default on the attribute), credentials attach to the actor directly as they already do (CM-54), and stored authorization policies disappear, so every "re-apply authorization after a write" step (CM-19, CM-38, CM-48) goes away and the visibility rules of CM-11..15, CM-37, CM-46/47 and CM-51 must be stated as read-time predicates on the actor — which is also where findings F-6 and F-7 (ungated root queries) must not be carried over. Kratos and the BFF session stay (ID-1/ID-2), so CM-01, CM-24, CM-28 and CM-30/31 are unchanged behaviours against an unchanged provider; account deletion and the admin identity operations move to the ops namespace (ID-4, slices 10–12) with the audit rows kept. Organisation verification becomes a `stage` on the organisation actor (CM-42); the model card (CM-52) is a property of the `ai_engine` registry rather than a resolver constant. Three facts found here should shape the model: (1) an organisation must be born with an owner (F-1) and the entry-role rule must be a constraint, not a flag (F-2); (2) e-mail verification and the duplicate-email check must be enforced at the actor boundary (F-3, F-4); (3) rename is one door (F-5).

## Findings (sources disagree, or likely defect)

1. **An organisation is born without an owner (CM-33, register E2).** Code: `createOrganization` assigns the creator ASSOCIATE + ADMIN, never OWNER (`organization.service.ts:204-221`); the role definition says owners 1..3 (`organization.role.definitions.ts:62-63`) and the floor is checked only on removal. Test: `organization-settings` pins three associates (creator, Admin, Associate) with no owner. Spec: 027 A6 names Support as creator; 054 US4 calls "an organisation always has an owner" an invariant the platform declares. ACC: 126 organisations with zero owners, 40 with neither owner nor admin, 11 of 23 organisations created in 2026 with no roles at all. Ruling: who owns a Support-created organisation, and must creation satisfy the floor?
2. **Admin / owner without the associate entry role (CM-34, register A6).** Code: `requiresEntryRole: true` on ADMIN and OWNER is read only by a display field (`role.set.resolver.fields.ts:245`); no service enforces it. Test: `organization-owner` › `should create organization owner` assigns OWNER to a user who is not an associate and passes — enshrining the gap. Spec 062 FR-019/US5-AS2 already plans around "an admin or owner who is not an associate". ACC: 757 credentials, 26 users. Ruling: enforce (auto-grant the entry role, or refuse), or drop the flag.
3. **E-mail verification is never checked on the live registration path (CM-01, register I4).** Code: `identity-resolve.service.ts:98-108` hard-codes `emailVerified: true` ("FIXME: temporary ugly workaround"), so `registerNewUser`'s "not verified" refusal (`registration.service.ts:74-79`) is unreachable. Test: none. Spec: 026-identity-consolidation assumes verified sign-ups; nothing states that unverified identities may hold profiles. ACC: 56 users whose identity e-mail is unverified. Ruling: defect, or an accepted consequence of the OIDC flow verifying upstream?
4. **Duplicate-email check is case-sensitive and runs before normalisation (CM-04).** Code: `validateUserProfileCreationRequest` looks the raw address up exactly (`user.service.ts:590-597`, `user.lookup.service.ts:69-80`) and lowercases afterwards (599); `Foo@x.io` against a stored `foo@x.io` passes the check and then fails on the `user.email` unique index with a raw database error. Test: the "twice" case hits the nameID check first. Ruling: normalise before the check (and rebaseline the message).
5. **Two rename doors (CM-18).** Code: `updateUser.nameID` (`user.service.ts:1081-1087`) and `updateVirtualContributor.nameID` (`virtual.contributor.service.ts:320-329`) change the URL path under plain UPDATE, which `platform-content-full-access` holds on every user and VC through the root content rule; `updateActorNameID` reserves it to `UPDATE_NAMEID` holders. Spec 027 A17: "no global role reaches it, Content Full Access included". Test: `platform-roles/rules/rename-nameid` proves the denial for spaces only. Ruling: strip `nameID` from the ordinary update inputs (schema-breaking) or gate it there too.
6. **Organisation reads are ungated (CM-37).** Code: `organization(ID)`, `organizations`, `organizationsPaginated` (`organization.resolver.queries.ts:17-57`) and `lookup.organization` (`lookup.resolver.fields.ts:195-203`) call no authorization check; `settings`, `verification`, `contactEmail`, `domain`, `legalEntityName`, `website` are plain fields; the stored policy grants READ to registered users and the matrix records anonymous as `·`. Test: all organisation reads run as the admin persona. Spec: none. Ruling: is an organisation's record (and its membership switches and verification state) public, or only its profile?
7. **`virtualContributor(ID)` is ungated (CM-51).** Code: the root query returns the VC to any caller (`virtual.contributor.resolver.queries.ts:46-56`); only engine / persona / model card / provider / status / authorization / knowledge base are field-gated, so `nameID`, `searchVisibility`, `listedInStore`, `bodyOfKnowledgeType`, `dataAccessMode`, `interactionModes` and `bodyOfKnowledgeDescription` of a hidden or account-scoped VC are readable by id; `lookup.virtualContributor` is gated READ. Test: the access tables use `lookup` (refused → `undefined`). Ruling: gate the root query like the lookup.
8. **Organisation deletion is not transactional (CM-36).** Code: profile, storage, groups, authorization, verification, role set, actor, then the account are deleted one after another (`organization.service.ts:383-413`, `registration.service.ts:656-664`) while user deletion runs one transaction; a failure mid-way leaves a half-deleted organisation — register E3 (43 accounts without owner) and E4 (3 verifications without organisation) have that shape. Test: none. Ruling: defect to fix in the legacy stack or accepted until the rebuild?
9. **`REJECT` needs no GRANT and the MANUALLY_VERIFY / RESET rows are parked on a 2022 bug (CM-42).** Code: every operator transition is guarded `hasGrantPrivilege` except `REJECT` (`...service.lifecycle.ts:584`), so an organisation's own admin can reject their own request; `organization-verification` comments its MANUALLY_VERIFY and RESET rows out "until fix is implemented for bug #1791" (server#1791, 2022), yet `organization-settings` runs MANUALLY_VERIFY successfully in its setup. Ruling: guard REJECT (or document it as the requester's own withdrawal) and restore the two rows.
10. **Chat identity survives deletion (CM-29, register P4).** Code: no deletion path deactivates the Matrix account; alkem-io/server#6728 is open (84 orphan Matrix users, 1,780 messages by deleted senders on ACC). Spec 054 records it as a v1 residual with a follow-up. Ruling: keep as the tracked defect; the rule stays "disputed" until the follow-up lands.
11. **`listedInStore` is dead for VCs (CM-45).** Code: set true at creation and settable on update, read by nothing (only packs and hubs are filtered on it). Test: none. Ruling: drop from the VC or wire the store.
12. **Domain lookup is exact-case (CM-10).** Code: `getOrganizationByDomain` matches the stored `domain` exactly (`organization.lookup.service.ts:66-74`) while the eligibility rule compares lower-case; an organisation whose domain was saved with capitals never auto-joins anyone. Test: domains are generated lower-case. Ruling: normalise the stored domain.
13. **Direct user creation makes identity-less users (CM-02, register E1/I1).** Code: `createUser` stores no `authenticationID` and skips the domain auto-join; such a user can never sign in until an identity with that e-mail appears. Test: every disposable user of the legacy suite (`create-user`, `delete-user`, `update-user`, `SRA-E1`) is this shape, and the harness relies on it. Spec: none mentions the mutation. ACC: 13 users with NULL `authenticationID`, 12 with no Kratos identity at all. Ruling: is an admin-provisioned profile a product feature or a harness convenience to retire?
14. **The knowledge-base-access oracle reads the wrong bucket for knowledge-based VCs (CM-47).** Test: in all four blocks the "VC knowledge base storageBucket › knowledgeBasedVC" table calls `queryVCStorageConfig` (the VC profile bucket) instead of `queryVCKnowledgeStorageConfig`; the 20 rows pass because the VC-bucket rule holds, so the KB-bucket visibility of a knowledge-based VC is unproven. Code/spec: no disagreement. Ruling: fix the test; no behaviour change.
